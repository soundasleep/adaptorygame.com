Help them survive

Your crew of four have crash-landed on a mysterious planetoid, deep in uncharted space. Using the planet’s limited resources and your creativity, you must keep your motley team alive, explore your surroundings, build a sustainable & thriving base, master a variety of physics simulations, rebuild your crashed ship, and ultimately get home!

Connect with your crew

Keep your explorers alive, and learn about their unique personalities from their conversations and diary entries.

Master complex simulations

Sophisticated physics and chemistry-based simulations with more than 50 elements, resulting in deep and emergent gameplay.

Adapt to the elements

Look out for solar flares, eclipses, meteor showers, auroras, and more! Randomly generated world events are triggered as your crew digs deeper.

Every playthrough is different!

Every world and explorer is procedurally generated, with numerous hidden spaces to discover.

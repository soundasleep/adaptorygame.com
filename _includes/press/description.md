## Pitch

A cozy and colourful 2D base-building/simulation game, combining multiple complex simulations with a diverse crew and story-rich world.

## Description

**Adaptory** is a 2D base-building simulation game set on a deserted planetoid deep in uncharted space.
Your motley crew of four explorers have crash-landed, and you need to keep them breathing, fed, sheltered, and satisfied.
Discover your team's eclectic personalities through conversations, journals, and emergent gameplay and storytelling, and watch the drama unfold – or dig deeper to unearth the planet's mysteries.
Will your crew complete their mission and ultimately find a new home?

## <a name="features"></a>Features

- The player is limited to eight explorers, so base design and automation becomes essential
- Explorers have conversations and diary entries so the player becomes more emotionally invested in their team
- Randomly generated world events are triggered as the player digs deeper
- Every world and explorer is procedurally generated, with traits, homelands, and personality types
- Sophisticated physics and chemistry simulations create deep and emergent gameplay
- Regular free content updates during Early Access
- Available for Windows and Mac

## <a name="history"></a>History

_Adaptory_ started as a prototype in February 2021.
Once a fun gameplay loop was identified,
_Stormcloak Games_ was incorporated in November 2021 to support full-time development of
Adaptory as their first title.

The [first playable Adaptory release](https://stormcloak.games/2022/04/30/first-playable-release)
was released to Patreons in April 2022, and then
[on itch.io](https://stormcloak.games/2022/10/30/download-alpha-1) in October 2022.
Regular alpha updates were then released
every two months [for free on itch.io](https://soundasleepful.itch.io/adaptory)
and most recently [Alpha 13 in September 2025](https://stormcloak.games/2025/09/03/alpha-13).

After being part of Steam Next Fest [in February 2024](https://stormcloak.games/2024/01/28/steam-next-fest),
Adaptory was [launched into Steam Early Access](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2201620/view/500596215477439779) in January 2026,
with the support of [publisher indie.io](https://stormcloak.games/2024/08/08/publisher-announcement).
The studio continues to release regular free content updates during Early Access,
including [the Materials Update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2201620/view/493847154103682361) in April, and
[the Temperature Update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2201620/view/674002554701480312) in August.

Adaptory and Stormcloak Games have been the recipient of the following grants and awards:
